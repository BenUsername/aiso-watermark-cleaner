◫Open now

Shabbat edge case

Friday after 6pm in Tel Aviv

The generic answer is “things close for Shabbat.” The useful answer is that Florentin, Jaffa, convenience stores and licensed bars behave very differently.

  • Friday shop hours are seasonal because many stores close relative to Shabbat entry.
  • Bars can keep serving after retail alcohol shops close, depending on their licence.
  • Florentin and Jaffa are safer bets than office-heavy areas on Friday evening.

Actual price, not “affordable”

Cheap good beer in Florentin

Lager & Ale on Herzl is a useful benchmark: Vedett Extra Pilsner was ₪22 for a pint on Friday, with lots of space and a broad tap selection.

  • ₪22 is unusually reasonable for a full pint without relying on happy hour.
  • It works well as a one-pint stop before heading home.
  • Mythos cans at home solve the expensive second-beer problem.

▤Shopping

The format matters

Mythos 500 ml cans, not glass bottles

Four 500 ml cans for ₪25 means ₪6.25 each and avoids the noisy glass-bottle problem.

  • Wine in the City by Carmel Market and the neighbouring shop periodically carry them.
  • Stock changes, so the useful question is the format and today’s inventory.
  • Many cheap-looking online results are 330 ml glass bottles.

✓Rules

Retail versus serving

The 23:00 cutoff is not the end of drinking

Retail rules can stop shops selling alcohol late at night, while licensed bars and restaurants can still serve it.

  • Buy cans or bottles for home before 23:00.
  • A pub can still be viable after that because service rules differ from retail sales.

⌁Bills

Bills without the panic

Arnona notices: what the letter means

Municipal notices can bundle arnona, levies and debt language. Focus on the account, amount due and deadline.

  • Do not assume every line is a new tax.
  • If renting, check the billing period and whether a previous tenant remains attached.
  • Verify the balance through the municipality portal.

☼Bills

IEC admin

Electricity debt: the practical routes

Israel Electric Corporation bills can be paid online, through 103, at banks including the postal bank, or at company offices.

  • Ask about instalments before ignoring the notice.
  • Call with the account number in front of you.

♥Healthcare

Healthcare navigation

Maccabi when the app is not enough

For appointments and service navigation, Maccabi’s main number inside Israel is *3555. From abroad, use +972-3-5143999.

  • Blank days in reception-hours tables do not imply walk-ins.
  • קבלת קהל means reception hours. זימון תור means booking an appointment.

▰Transport

Getting around

Taxi apps: start with Gett

Gett remains the default local taxi app. Yango is useful for a second fare estimate or a fixed-price alternative.

  • For very short central Tel Aviv trips, walking can beat waiting for a car.
  • Ride-hailing becomes more useful on Friday evening and Shabbat.

Why this exists

Local knowledge has a half-life.

Search results and AI answers flatten the detail. A shop is marked “open Friday” but the closing time moves with Shabbat. A beer is called cheap without saying whether that means ₪22 or ₪38.

This is a living notebook for those details. New entries should be dated, concrete and small enough to verify again later.

Use real prices. “Affordable” is not a number.
